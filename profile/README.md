# Myrado Studio

Custom AI agents, chatbots, voice agents and AI automation for small and mid-sized businesses. A senior engineering studio from Kyiv, Ukraine.

## What we build

- **Chat agents** for websites, WhatsApp, Instagram and Telegram: they answer from your knowledge base, qualify leads, book appointments and update your CRM.
- **Voice agents** that answer calls in a natural voice and hand difficult calls to a person.
- **Back-office automation** with AI steps on n8n: invoices into your books, lead scoring, reports, inbox triage and call QA.
- **Knowledge-base assistants (RAG)** that answer staff questions with citations.
- **MCP servers and Claude integrations** for internal tools.

## How we work

- A working demo on your data before the main invoice.
- Fixed price with written acceptance criteria.
- You own the code, workflows and accounts.
- 30 days of tuning after launch are included.

## Open source

- [checkbox-mcp](https://github.com/myradostudio/checkbox-mcp) — an unofficial MCP server for the Checkbox ПРРО API (Ukrainian software cash register): receipts, shifts, X/Z reports, goods and orders for AI assistants. Read-only by default.
- [n8n-templates](https://github.com/myradostudio/n8n-templates) — three free n8n workflow templates: a daily Prozorro tender digest with AI summaries, invoice photos and PDFs to Google Sheets with an arithmetic check, and Nova Poshta parcel status changes to Telegram.
